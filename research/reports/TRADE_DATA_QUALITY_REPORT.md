# TRADE_DATA_QUALITY_REPORT

Generated by `scripts/validate_market_data.py` and `scripts/build_market_data.py` from Databento trades (GLBX.MDP3). Every trade is checked; fatal findings stop the pipeline, findings to review stay in the data and are handled in the `DATA_QUALITY <dataset id>` journal note. The bar-level report of the registered datasets is `reports/DATA_QUALITY_REPORT.md`.

_No Databento data has been validated yet._
